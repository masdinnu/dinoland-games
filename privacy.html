BULLSEYE RIVALS · Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026 · com.bullseyerivals.game

Dinoland develops BULLSEYE RIVALS. This policy explains how the Android game handles information and how you can manage your privacy choices.

Game data on your device

The game stores your chosen nickname and avatar, equipment, coins, XP, settings, tutorial state, progress, match history, personal records, achievements and reward records in the app’s local storage. The game uses these records to restore your progress and prevent duplicate rewards. The game has no online account or Dinoland cloud-save service and does not send your nickname, match history or save files to a Dinoland server. Core gameplay can be played offline.

Advertising and Google AdMob

The Android game integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK for AdMob. When an ad request is allowed and an ad is ready, an interstitial may appear at the end of a match or session. The game tracks eligible button actions and arrow releases locally during the session to decide when an interstitial can appear. This counter is not sent to Google by the game. Rewarded ads are optional. The Coin Store grants 100 in-game coins when Google reports that you earned the reward, and the grant is recorded locally. The game does not require you to watch an ad to play.

Google's SDK may process IP addresses, device or account identifiers including the Android advertising ID where available, interactions with ads or the app, and diagnostics. Google may use this data to deliver and measure ads, perform analytics and help prevent fraud. An IP address may be used to estimate general location. The game does not request access to precise device location.

Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Consent and privacy choices

The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update consent status and present privacy messages where applicable. The UMP check may contact Google before any ad request is made. The game requests ads only after a successful UMP update reports a status that permits them. When UMP requires a privacy entry point, open Settings → Privacy Options to review or change your choices. You can also manage your advertising ID through Android’s privacy settings.

Retention, deletion and requests

Local game records remain on your device while the app retains its data. You can remove them through Android’s app storage settings or by uninstalling the app. Clearing app data removes local progress, coins and settings. Google’s handling and retention of SDK data are described in its privacy policy. You can email Dinoland with questions or requests about your information. If you contact us, we use the email address and information you choose to send to respond and handle your request.
